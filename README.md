# Building Energy Performance Simulation

**Course:** 7B2M0 – Building Performance and Energy Systems Simulation  
**University:** Eindhoven University of Technology  
**Tools:** DesignBuilder, EnergyPlus

This repository contains two course assignment reports exploring building energy performance simulation.

### Assignment 1 — BESTEST Validation
Modeled a reference building in DesignBuilder, simulated heating and cooling energy demands, and compared the results against BESTEST benchmarks.

### Assignment 2 — Virtual Laboratory Experiment
Investigated transient heat transfer through insulation materials using DesignBuilder/EnergyPlus, comparing simulation results with analytical solutions and evaluating the effects of simulation time-step resolution.
